MachineNova has been deployed across regional and national campaign operations. The following scenarios represent the types of deployments our platforms support.

Regional Deployment Mobilizer Platform

Regional Party Mobilization Deployment

A regional party deployed the Mobilizer Platform across 12 constituencies during a campaign period, replacing manual spreadsheet tracking with ID-verified field data.

The Challenge

The campaign had no reliable way to measure actual voter engagement in the field. Mobilizer reports were anecdotal, unverified, and inconsistent across constituencies. Leadership had no real-time visibility into coverage gaps or high-performing areas.

The Deployment

The Mobilizer Platform was deployed across 12 constituencies with 48 appointed mobilizers. Each mobilizer was issued credentials and used the app to scan voter IDs during door-to-door engagement. Every capture was attributed to the mobilizer and synced to the central database in real time.

40,000+ID-verified voter contacts captured in 3 weeks
12Constituencies with full mobilizer attribution
Real-TimeCoverage gap visibility for campaign leadership
National Deployment Election Operations Command Center

National Agent Coordination Rollout

The Election Operations Command Center was deployed across 329 polling stations, giving national leadership live visibility into station status, turnout, and results from a single dashboard.

The Challenge

The campaign had agents at hundreds of polling stations but no structured way to receive, verify, and aggregate their reports. Results were being called in by phone, creating delays, errors, and disputes about accuracy.

The Deployment

The Command Center was deployed with a four-level hierarchy: national administrator, regional administrators, constituency managers, and polling-station agents. Agents submitted station status, turnout updates, and results through the platform. Managers verified submissions before aggregation. National leadership monitored everything from a single live dashboard.

329Polling stations monitored in real time
4-LevelCommand hierarchy with role-based access
LiveResult aggregation as counting progressed
Event Intelligence Event Intelligence Platform

Political Rally Intelligence Pilot

The Event Intelligence Platform was used at a major political rally, giving organisers real-time data on registered voters in attendance, regional breakdown, and KYC verification — all at the gate.

The Challenge

The campaign wanted to understand not just how many people attended the rally, but how many were registered voters in the host constituency — and how many were visiting from other regions. This data was critical for planning follow-up mobilization.

The Deployment

The guest list was uploaded to the system in advance. At the entrance, each attendee's national ID was scanned. The system performed real-time voter lookup, KYC verification, and regional classification. Organisers had a live dashboard showing total check-ins, registered voter count, and regional breakdown throughout the event.
