01 Political Event Intelligence

Event Intelligence
Platform

Know exactly who walked through the door — and what that means for your campaign.

Built for rallies, aspirant forums, and internal party gatherings. Upload your guest list, scan national IDs at the entrance, and get a real-time political picture of every attendee. The application is especially valuable for measuring the political reach of an event: organisers can see not only how many people attended, but how many are registered to vote in the relevant electoral area.

ID Verification

Scans the national ID at the entrance and confirms the attendee's identity against the loaded guest list.

Voter Lookup

Indicates whether the attendee is a registered voter and identifies the region in which they are registered.

KYC Verification

Lightweight Know-Your-Customer verification on the attendee's submitted details against trusted records.

Live Statistics

Running totals on total check-ins, registered voters, unregistered attendees, and a regional breakdown.

Region Filtering

Highlights attendees who are registered voters in the region hosting the event versus those visiting from elsewhere.

Mobilization Intelligence

Attendance data directly informs follow-up mobilization efforts in the relevant electoral area.

02 Ground Campaign Infrastructure

Mobilizer
Platform

Replace anecdotal estimates with ID-verified, defensible field data.

A campaign-period tool used to coordinate door-to-door and community mobilization efforts. Each appointed mobilizer is issued credentials and uses the app to scan the national IDs of voters they have engaged. Every scan attributes that voter to the mobilizer who recruited them, producing a transparent record of mobilization performance.

Voter Capture

Mobilizers scan voter IDs as they engage citizens, instantly capturing each contact into the central database.

Attribution

Every voter captured is linked to the specific mobilizer who recruited them, providing accountability across the field team.

Performance Metrics

Per-mobilizer counts so the campaign can identify high performers and areas that need additional support.

Vote Projection

Aggregated mobilization data gives a grounded, ID-verified estimate of likely votes in each area.

Regional Coverage

Maps mobilization activity geographically so campaign leadership can identify gaps and over-covered areas.

Real-Time Sync

All captures sync to the central database in real time, visible to campaign leadership immediately.

03 Flagship Platform

Election Operations
Command Center

The operational backbone of election day. Total visibility from national command to individual polling stations.

The most comprehensive platform in the MachineNova suite. It manages the full chain of command — from national administrators down to individual polling-station agents — and captures and aggregates results as they are reported from the field.

Structure & Deployment

Role-Based Access

Administrators, regional administrators, managers and agents each have permissions calibrated to their responsibility.

Geographic Scoping

Managers can be restricted to a specific region, constituency or ward, ensuring they only oversee their assigned area.

Agent Directory

A single interface lists all appointed agents and managers, with contact details readily available for command and coordination.

Add / Remove Users

Administrators can onboard or remove managers and agents as the structure evolves before and during the election.

Election-Day Monitoring

Station Status

Confirms which polling stations have opened on time, based on direct reports from agents at each location.

Turnout Tracking

Hourly or two-hourly breakdowns of voter turnout by polling centre, region and constituency.

Centralised Visibility

All field activity in a single dashboard, allowing senior leadership to intervene where attention is needed.

Real-Time Results

Live Tallying

Results flow from agents to managers and into the portal, where they are aggregated as they arrive.

Leaderboard View

Shows in real time which candidates are leading or trailing across each contested race.

Manager Verification

Managers serve as the verification layer, keying in agent submissions to ensure data quality before aggregation.

Visual Dashboards

Maps, charts and headline figures that update continuously as the count progresses.

04 Polling Intelligence & Strategy

Analytics &
Intelligence

Strategic intelligence that informs campaign planning and election-day strategy.

Alongside the operational platforms, MachineNova offers a research and analytics capability that supports strategic decision-making. The team conducts surveys and processes voter registration and demographic data to produce regional intelligence.

Registration Analysis

Quantifies, by region, how many citizens are registered to vote and how many remain unregistered.

Demographic Profiling

Identifies the dominant communities in a particular region and other demographic dynamics relevant to political strategy.

Voter Breakdown

Region-by-region analysis of the voter population, useful for targeting both mobilization and campaign messaging.

Strategic Insight

Combines field data with survey work to surface the political dynamics at play in each region.

Turnout Trends

Historical and real-time turnout analysis to identify high-priority areas for mobilization focus.

Survey Intelligence

Structured survey capability to gather voter sentiment and political preference data at scale.
