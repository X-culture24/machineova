01

Event Check-In
Application

Built for rallies, aspirant forums, and internal party gatherings. Upload your guest list, scan national IDs at the entrance, and get a real-time political picture of every attendee.

  • ID Verification — Scans the national ID and confirms identity against the loaded guest list instantly.
  • Voter Lookup — Indicates whether the attendee is a registered voter and identifies their region.
  • KYC Checks — Lightweight Know-Your-Customer verification against trusted records in real time.
  • Live Statistics & Region Filtering — Running totals on check-ins, registered voters, and a live regional breakdown.

02

Mobilizer
Application

A campaign-period tool for coordinating door-to-door mobilization. Every voter scanned is attributed to the mobilizer who recruited them — replacing anecdotal estimates with ID-verified records.

  • Voter Capture — Mobilizers scan voter IDs, instantly capturing each contact into the central database.
  • Attribution — Every voter linked to the specific mobilizer who recruited them — full accountability.
  • Performance Metrics — Per-mobilizer counts identify high performers and areas needing support.
  • Vote Projection & Regional Coverage — ID-verified estimate of likely votes and coverage gap maps.

03

Agents Management
& Results Portal

The operational backbone of election day. Manages the full chain of command from national administrators down to individual polling-station agents, and captures results live.

  • Role-Based Access — Four permission levels calibrated to each role in the hierarchy.
  • Geographic Scoping — Managers restricted to their specific region, constituency, or ward.
  • Agent Directory — Single interface listing all agents and managers with contact details.
  • Station Status — Confirms which polling stations have opened on time from agent reports.
  • Turnout Tracking — Hourly breakdowns of voter turnout by polling centre, region, and constituency.
  • Centralised Visibility — All field activity in one dashboard so leadership can intervene where needed.
  • Live Tallying — Results flow from agents to managers and into the portal as they arrive.
  • Leaderboard View — Real-time view of which candidates are leading or trailing across each race.
  • Visual Dashboards — Maps, charts, and headline figures updating continuously as counting progresses.

Frequently
Asked Questions

All four platforms share a common identity, voter, and reporting layer. Data captured by a mobilizer in the field is immediately visible in the national dashboard without any manual transfer.

The platform is built around national ID scanning. Specific document types are configured during onboarding to match the identity infrastructure of your country or region.

The platform is designed for real-time connectivity. Our team works with clients during setup to assess field connectivity and recommend appropriate data plans or offline contingency approaches.

The Agents Portal uses role-based access control with four levels: national administrator, regional administrator, manager, and agent. Managers can be geographically scoped to a specific region, constituency, or ward.

The platform scales from local constituency campaigns up to national elections. You can deploy one platform or the full suite depending on your operational needs.
