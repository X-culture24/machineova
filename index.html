Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Questions about the MachineNova platform suite. Can't find what you need?

Talk to our team →

All four platforms share a common identity, voter, and reporting layer. Data captured by a mobilizer in the field is immediately visible in the national command dashboard without any manual transfer or aggregation.

The platform is built around national ID scanning. Specific document types are configured during onboarding to match the identity infrastructure of your country or region.

The Command Center uses role-based access control with four levels: national administrator, regional administrator, manager, and agent. Managers can be geographically scoped to a specific region, constituency, or ward.

The platform scales from local constituency campaigns up to national elections. You can deploy one platform or the full suite depending on your operational needs.

Our team works with clients during setup to assess field connectivity and recommend appropriate data plans or contingency approaches for low-connectivity environments.
