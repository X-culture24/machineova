Inside the Platform

See What Your Team Sees on Election Day

Real screens from the MachineNova workspace. Every view is scoped to the user's role and geography, so a ward manager sees their four stations and the county owner sees all of them.

What needs you now

The overview opens on the four numbers that matter on the day: reporting progress, stations open, turnout so far and how many stations need attention.

  • Needs-attention list: KIEMS kit failures, absent agents, uncovered stations and results waiting for verification, each with a one-click action.
  • Turnout trend: a cumulative curve for the whole scope, updated as agents report.

Every station, one drawer away

Filter stations by status (Open, Reporting, Issue, Closed, Uncovered) and open any one to act on it without leaving the list.

  • Update status, assign an agent from the ward pool, record turnout, report an incident.
  • Call or WhatsApp the assigned agent in one tap.
  • A timestamped activity log for every change.

Provisional results, per race

Switch between Governor, Senator, Woman Rep, MP and MCA. See who leads, by what share, and how much of each constituency has reported, then drill down to ward and station.

  • Figures are entered from the signed Form 37A and checked against recorded turnout.
  • Clearly labelled as provisional agent-reported tallies, not an official declaration.

Check a voter in, in five taps

Pick the event, type or scan the ID, and the platform resolves the voter from the register: name, sex, date of birth and registered polling centre.

  • Flags voters from outside the event's region without blocking them.
  • List-only events admit only approved participants.
  • Duplicates are caught: "Already checked in" shows when and by whom.

Know where to push next

Coverage compares check-ins with the voters available in each constituency, ward and polling centre, and rates every area from Strong to Critical.

  • Push here next suggests where effort will do the most good.
  • Gender mix and top age group for everyone reached.

Checkin Field App

Built for the Field. Big Targets, Works Offline.

Field agents and mobilizers run the Android app with ID scanning, 44px+ tap targets and offline support. Mobilization and check-in keep working without signal and sync later. Teal means check-in, amber means mobilize, so the job is obvious at a glance.

Flagship Platform

Election Operations
Command Center

The most comprehensive platform in the MachineNova suite. Manages the full chain of command from national administrators down to individual polling-station agents — and captures results live as counting begins.

  • Role-based access: Owner, Executive, Admin, Chief Agent, Manager and Agent
  • Geographic scoping to county, constituency or ward
  • Live polling station status: Open, Reporting, Issue, Closed, Uncovered
  • Cumulative turnout by time slot, rolled up hourly by region
  • Form 37A result entry with turnout cross-checks
  • Election replay: scrub back through archived election days hour by hour
